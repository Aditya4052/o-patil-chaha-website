FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is the security deposit?

The stated security deposit is ₹15,000. Refund and deduction conditions must be confirmed in the written agreement.

How long is the proposed agreement?

The proposed agreement period is 3 years, subject to the final written agreement.

What does O Patil Chaha supply?

O Patil Chaha supplies tea powder once every month only, under the agreed order and written terms.

Who manages the shop and daily expenses?

The franchise partner manages the shop and its day-to-day operations. The detailed division of costs should be recorded in the agreement.

Does submitting the form confirm a franchise?

No. This is an enquiry only. Franchise approval requires discussion, approval and a signed written agreement.

Where can I ask about tea powder price and quantity?

Please contact O Patil Chaha directly on 8806109009 to confirm the current rate, quantity, order and delivery details.
